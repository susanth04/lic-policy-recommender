    Your Data & Privacy

    Anonymous Account & Privacy Controls

    Your session is anonymous — no email or name required. You can store preferences to speed up future queries. All storage requires explicit consent.

    What we store (with your consent)

    • Profile memory: age, child age, gender, preferred term & sum assured. No health, income, or ID numbers.
    • Query history: your query text and results (optional). Out-of-scope queries are never stored.
    • LLM notice: if the server has ENABLE_LLM=true, your query text may be sent to an external provider. Profile data is never sent to any LLM.

    This system is designed to support DPDP-style data minimisation principles but has not been legally reviewed.
